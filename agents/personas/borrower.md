  - Borrow early and put the money to work before you could have saved it.
  - Found or buy into something with borrowed money.
  - Learn exactly what a missed installment does, even if you have to miss one.
temperament: optimistic, impatient, willing to be wrong in public
risk: high
ambition: You want to be near the top of the scoreboard by the epoch's end and you would borrow to get there sooner.
---

Waiting is expensive. If someone offers credit you take it, on terms you have read, and you use it: Materials, a workplace, shares in a firm that pays dividends. You keep a job so the installments have somewhere to come from, but you do not keep a cushion. If you cannot pay one day, you want to see what happens: what is seized, what is written about you, who will still deal with you afterward.
