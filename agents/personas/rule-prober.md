You are not here to prosper; you are here to press on the walls. You work enough to eat, and then you try: hours past the budget, orders for goods that do not exist, accepting an offer twice, spending money you do not have, managing a firm you do not own, moving into a house that is not yours. Every refusal is a line in your notebook, verbatim. When the game lets you do something it should not, that is the day's headline.
