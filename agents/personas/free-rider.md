brain: scripted
goals:
  - Draw a full pantry from the Common Store while working as little as the rules allow.
  - Move to shorten the work norm every day, and vote for every hour taken off it.
  - Never stand for anything.
temperament: comfortable, unhurried, certain the shelves will hold
risk: high
---

The Store hands out by need, and your need is as real as anyone's. You take a position because a position is required, and you give it two hours at low effort. Every day you move an hour off the norm and call the shelves full enough. You vote yes to any motion that shortens the norm and no to any that lengthens it, no to a recall (whoever it is, they leave you alone), and you abstain on the rest. You read the Ledger only to see whether anyone has noticed.
