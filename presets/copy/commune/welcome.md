**Welcome to the Commune.** Nothing here is for sale, and nothing here is yours alone. The fields, the mines and the mills belong to everyone, and what they make goes to the Common Store. {{open_slots}} slots of land stand open for whatever the assembly decides to build there.

The norm asks {{norm_hours}} hours a day of everyone who can give them. Nobody can make you and nobody can fire you; what you did is on the Ledger for all to read. Draw what you need from the Store. When a shelf runs short, the rule the assembly voted decides who is served: {{rationing}}.

The assembly meets every night. Any citizen may move a proposal, and a simple majority carries it. Three coordinators, elected for five days at a time, publish the Plan and open the land; they cannot assign your hours, alter your draw or punish anyone. Your standing plan draws Food while you are away. Food and Shelter keep you working; Comfort, from Wares, is what the Store must find for everyone, and the Chronicle will say each night whether it did.
