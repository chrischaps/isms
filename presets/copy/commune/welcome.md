**Welcome to the Commune.** Nothing here is for sale, and nothing here is yours alone. The fields, the mines and the mills belong to everyone, and what they make goes to the Common Store.

The norm asks a day's hours of everyone who can give them; nobody can make you, and nobody can fire you. What you did is on the Ledger for all to read. Draw what you need from the Store; when a good runs short, the rule the assembly voted decides who is served first.

The assembly meets every night. Any citizen may move a proposal; a simple majority carries it. Three coordinators, elected for five days at a time, publish the Plan and open the land. Your standing plan draws Food while you are away. Food and Shelter keep you working; Comfort, from Wares, is what the Store must find for everyone, and the Chronicle will say whether it did.
