The five layers

Every screen sorts its content into these layers. Nothing is removed — each datum gets a level. Layer 3 is the "no information lost" guarantee and is always visible.

1 · Verdictone sentence
You're well fed and working today, but you don't have a place to live yet.
2 · Glance≤ 6 items, each with a status line
Food 100 — "Full. You eat 1 food every hour from your pantry." · Shelter 94 — "Falling 2 an hour — you have no dwelling."
3 · Factsalways visible
Balance 967.25 cr · Pantry 22 food · Dwelling none · Work Legacy Farm No. 1, 8 h, normal · payslip · plan · Chronicle · footer figures
4 · Detailone labelled tap away
"More about today's work": hour budget 8 h, effort ×1.0, food decay ×1.0, output multiplier ×0.70, the two-workplace rule.
5 · Explainon demand, per number or term
? → "Shelter only drops while you're unhoused. Rent or buy a dwelling on the Contracts screen and it climbs back."
Do write the Verdict first. If it can't be written, the screen's purpose isn't clear yet.
Don't add a density toggle that hides layer 3. Detail collapses; facts never do.

Mobile first

Designed at 390px and grown. Breakpoints sm 480 · md 720 · lg 960. Below md every grid stacks, the screen nav becomes a five-slot bottom tab bar, tables with more than three columns become card-per-row, and every interactive element is at least 44px tall.

Card · Verdict · NeedCard

A card is --surface with a --line border and no shadow. Its status is carried by its content, never by tinting the card. The first card on a screen holds the Verdict and the Glance layer.

Right now

Food

Full. You eat 1 food every hour from your pantry.
Food is your survival meter. It drops about 4 points an hour and each food you eat puts 4 back. Below 50 you work slower; below 20 for a whole day counts as hardship.
Shelter
94 / 100
Falling 2 an hour — you have no dwelling.
Shelter only drops while you're unhoused. Rent or buy a dwelling on the Contracts screen and it climbs back. Being unhoused also cuts your work output by 30 %.
Comfort
14 / 100
Very low. Buy Wares on the Market to bring it back.
Comfort is the "nice things" meter. It never affects how much you produce, but it drives your wellbeing score and it's what buying Wares is for.

Voice and copy

Two voices. The shell (labels, verdicts, explainers, buttons, errors) is neutral, second person, present tense. The society (Chronicle, Welcome Brief, empty states) is the preset's and may be partisan. Neither leaks into the other.

ElementShapeExample
Verdict[good], and [good], but [the one thing to act on]. One "but" max.You're well fed and working today, but you don't have a place to live yet.
Status lineWhat's happening and, if changing, the rate.Falling 2 an hour — you have no dwelling.
Explain noteWhat it is · what moves it (real numbers from Params) · what to do. ≤ 3 sentences.Shelter only drops while you're unhoused. Rent or buy a dwelling on the Contracts screen and it climbs back. Being unhoused also cuts your work output by 30 %.
GlossTranslate one figure per screen into lived terms.22 food (about 22 hours)
ButtonVerb first, outcome named.Keep my plan · Set my hours · Take this job · Place bid
ConfirmationName the consequence instead of "Are you sure?"Found Iron & Sons for 200.00 cr — you'll have 762.01 cr after.
ErrorEngine's rejection verbatim, then what to change.You asked for 9 hours; your budget today is 8. — Set 8 or fewer.

Don't "Food meter: 100/100 (satisfied)." — the meter's words.
Do "Keep my plan" — what happens.
Don't "OK" / "Submit" — nothing named.
Do keep the shell neutral in every society.
